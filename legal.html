Terms and Privacy

Pilot version. CabLink is in a pilot phase in Botswana. These plain-language terms apply during the pilot and will be updated.

What CabLink does

CabLink connects passengers with independent drivers. CabLink does not own the cars and is not the driver. Drivers are checked and approved by CabLink before they can take rides.

Fares and payment

The fare is shown before you request a ride and is calculated by CabLink from the distance. You pay the driver directly, in cash or by mobile money to the driver's number.

Your information

We keep your name, phone number, trips and ratings so the service can work and be kept safe. Your phone number is shared with the other person on a trip only while that trip is active, so you can call each other. We do not sell your information. Your location is used only when you tap to use it.

Safety

In an emergency call the police on 999 or an ambulance on 997. The Safety button in a trip lets you share your trip and alert CabLink. Please report any problem so we can act on it.

Your responsibilities

Give true information, keep your PIN private, treat drivers and passengers with respect, and do not misuse the service. Accounts may be suspended for fraud, abuse or unsafe behaviour.

Contact

For questions or to ask us to correct or delete your information, contact CabLink through the details given by your pilot coordinator.
