Terms and Privacy

Pilot version. CabLink is in a pilot phase in Botswana. These plain-language terms apply during the pilot and will be updated.

What CabLink does

CabLink connects passengers with independent drivers. CabLink does not own the cars and is not the driver. Drivers are checked and approved by CabLink before they can take rides.

Fares and payment

The fare is shown before you request a ride and is calculated by CabLink from the distance. You pay the driver directly, in cash or by mobile money to the driver's number.

Your information

CabLink is run by BSTM in Botswana. We collect your name, phone number, PIN (stored only as a scrambled hash), trips, ratings, reward wallet address and, only when you tap to use it, your location. We use it to run rides, keep people safe, pay rewards and prevent fraud. Your phone number is shared with the other person on a trip only while that trip is active so you can call each other. We do not sell your information.

Where it is kept and for how long

Your data is stored on secure cloud services that may be hosted outside Botswana. We keep your account while it is active. Trip and reward records are kept as long as needed for safety, disputes and accounting, then removed or anonymised.

Your rights

Under the Botswana Data Protection Act 2024 you can ask us to show you, correct or delete your information, and to stop using it for a purpose you object to. Use the Support button in the app to make a request. If you are not satisfied, you may complain to the Information and Data Protection Commission.

By creating an account you confirm you have read these terms and agree to us using your information as described. We record when you accepted.

Safety

In an emergency call the police on 999 or an ambulance on 997. The Safety button in a trip lets you share your trip and alert CabLink. Please report any problem so we can act on it.

Your responsibilities

Give true information, keep your PIN private, treat drivers and passengers with respect, and do not misuse the service. Accounts may be suspended for fraud, abuse or unsafe behaviour.

Contact

For questions or to ask us to correct or delete your information, contact CabLink through the details given by your pilot coordinator.
